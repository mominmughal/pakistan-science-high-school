About the School

A science-first education, rooted in Sialkot.

Pakistan Science High School is located on Quaid-e-Azam Street, Hamza Ghouse, Sialkot. We provide a disciplined, science-focused environment for students working toward their matriculation, with small class sizes and close attention from subject teachers.

Our aim is simple: build a strong foundation in the sciences and mathematics, while keeping every student's character, discipline and results in view.

  • Qualified subject specialists for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths & Computer Science
  • Regular tests, parent-teacher contact and result tracking
  • Safe, disciplined campus with a focus on attendance and conduct

Academic Programs

Subjects our students major in.

PHY

Physics

Mechanics, waves, electricity and modern physics, taught with regular lab demonstration.

CHEM

Chemistry

Organic, inorganic and physical chemistry fundamentals, built for board exam success.

BIO

Biology

Human biology, botany and zoology, with diagram-based practice for exams.

MATH

Mathematics

Algebra, geometry and trigonometry with weekly problem-solving practice.

CS

Computer Science

Computer fundamentals and basic programming to prepare students for a digital future.

ENG

English & Urdu

Language and communication skills built alongside the core science curriculum.
